**Hi there, I'm Kshitij.**

I am a researcher (CSE) at [mathLab](https://mathlab.sissa.it/), [SISSA](https://www.sissa.it/), Trieste, Italy. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Department of Mathematics and Computer Science](https://www.dimai.unifi.it), [University of Florence](https://www.unifi.it), Italy. I completed my Ph.D. at the [Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/).

My research interests include Approximation Theory, Numerical Analysis, and Splines. Lately, I've started exploring Reduced-Order Modeling. For more details, please visit my [Personal Webpage](https://kshitij-maths.github.io).
